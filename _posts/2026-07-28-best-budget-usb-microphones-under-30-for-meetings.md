# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget USB microphone under 30, there are several key factors to consider. First, look for a microphone with a good frequency response, which refers to the range of frequencies that the mic can pick up. A wider frequency response will result in clearer and more accurate sound. Second, consider the type of connector the microphone uses - in this case, we're looking for USB connectivity for easy plug-and-play use. Third, think about the microphone's noise cancellation capabilities, as this will help to reduce background noise and ensure that your voice comes through clearly. Finally, check the microphone's durability and build quality, as a well-made mic will last longer and withstand regular use.
