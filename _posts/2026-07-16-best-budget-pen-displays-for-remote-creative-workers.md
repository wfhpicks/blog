#

# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget drawing tablet display, there are several key factors to consider. First, consider the screen size and resolution, as a larger and higher-resolution screen can provide a more immersive and accurate drawing experience. Second, think about the stylus and its accuracy, as a high-quality stylus can make a big difference in your ability to create precise lines and details. Third, look at the connectivity options, as a device that can connect to your computer via USB, HDMI, or wireless can provide more flexibility and convenience. Finally, consider the software compatibility, as some budget pen displays may only work with certain operating systems or programs.
