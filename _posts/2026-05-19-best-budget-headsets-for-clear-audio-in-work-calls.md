# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget headset for calls, there are several key factors to consider. First, look for a headset with a good quality microphone that can pick up your voice clearly. A noise-cancelling microphone is ideal, as it can help reduce background noise and ensure that your voice comes through clearly. Second, consider the comfort and durability of the headset. A comfortable headset with padded ear cups and an adjustable headband can make a big difference in long work calls. Third, think about the connectivity options - do you need a wireless headset or a wired one? Finally, check the compatibility of the headset with your device - whether it's a computer, phone, or tablet.
