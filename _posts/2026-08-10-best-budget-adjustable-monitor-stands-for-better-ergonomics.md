# What to Look For

In a budget monitor stand adjustable, there are several key factors to consider. First, the stand's adjustability is crucial; it should allow for a wide range of height and possibly tilt adjustments to accommodate different working positions and preferences. Second, stability is vital; the stand must be sturdy enough to hold your monitor securely without wobbling or tipping over. Third, compatibility is another important aspect; ensure the stand can support the weight and size of your monitor. Lastly, consider the material and build quality; while you're looking for a cheap monitor riser, it should still be durable and long-lasting.
