#

# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget white noise machine, there are several key factors to consider. First, think about the types of sounds you want to generate - do you prefer traditional white noise, or would you like more options like fan sounds or nature sounds? Second, consider the device's volume range and adjustability, as well as its overall sound quality. Third, look at the machine's design and size - a compact and portable device can be a big plus if you have limited space. Finally, check if the machine has any additional features, such as a timer, alarm, or USB port for charging your devices.
