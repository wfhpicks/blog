# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget monitor under 150, there are several key factors to consider. First, resolution is crucial - a 1080p monitor is the minimum you should aim for, as it provides a decent level of detail and clarity. Second, screen size is important - a 22-24 inch monitor is a good starting point, as it offers a comfortable viewing experience without taking up too much space. Third, connectivity options are essential - look for a monitor with multiple ports, such as HDMI, VGA, and DVI, to ensure you can connect it to your computer or other devices. Finally, consider the monitor's ergonomics - a adjustable stand and VESA mounting compatibility can make a big difference in terms of comfort and flexibility.

# Budget Tips

To get the best value from your budget monitor under 150, consider the following tips. First, look for monitors with a 1080p resolution, as they provide a good balance between price and picture quality. Second, consider a monitor with a TN panel, as they are often cheaper than IPS panels but still offer good performance. Third, check the monitor's connectivity options and make sure they match your needs - if you only need to connect one device, a monitor with fewer ports may be a more affordable option.
