# What to Look For

When searching for a budget keyboard mac, there are several key factors to consider. First, look for a keyboard with a compact tenkeyless design, which can help save space on your desk while still providing a comfortable typing experience. Second, consider the type of switch used in the keyboard, as this can affect the overall typing feel and noise level. Third, ensure the keyboard is compatible with your MacBook, with features like Bluetooth connectivity or a reliable USB connection. Finally, think about the ergonomics and build quality of the keyboard, as a well-designed keyboard can help reduce fatigue and improve productivity.
