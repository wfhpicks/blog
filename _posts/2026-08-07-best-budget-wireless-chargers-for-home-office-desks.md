#

# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget wireless charger desk, there are several key factors to consider. First, look for a charger that supports the Qi standard, which is the most widely adopted wireless charging protocol. This ensures compatibility with most smartphones and other devices. Second, consider the charging speed, with faster chargers typically supporting up to 10W or 15W charging. Third, think about the design and build quality, opting for a charger with a sturdy base and a smooth, sleek finish. Finally, check the price, as budget wireless chargers can range from under $10 to over $50, depending on the features and quality.
