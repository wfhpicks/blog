#

# What to Look For

When it comes to choosing the best budget blue light glasses, there are several key factors to consider. Firstly, look for glasses that block at least 90% of blue light in the 400-450nm range, which is the most harmful to our eyes. Secondly, consider the lens material and quality, as some cheap screen glasses may have poor optical clarity or distortions. Thirdly, think about the frame style and comfort, as you'll likely be wearing these glasses for extended periods. Lastly, check if the glasses have any additional features, such as anti-reflective coating or prescription lens options.
