# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget HDMI cable, there are several key factors to consider. First, consider the length of the cable - do you need a short cable for a compact setup or a longer one for a more extensive home office? Second, think about the version of HDMI you need - HDMI 2.0 is a good starting point, but if you need higher resolutions or faster refresh rates, you may need HDMI 2.1. Third, check the material and build quality of the cable - a well-made cable with good shielding can reduce interference and ensure a stable connection. Finally, consider any additional features you may need, such as Ethernet or audio return channel support.
