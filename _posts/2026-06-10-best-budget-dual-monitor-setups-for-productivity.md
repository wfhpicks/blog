# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget dual monitor setup, there are several key factors to consider. Firstly, resolution is crucial, as it will impact the clarity and sharpness of your screens. Look for monitors with a high resolution, such as Full HD or 4K, to ensure you can see every detail clearly. Secondly, consider the size of your monitors, as this will affect your overall viewing experience. Larger monitors can be beneficial for multitasking, but may take up more desk space. Thirdly, think about connectivity options, such as HDMI, DisplayPort, or USB-C, to ensure your monitors are compatible with your computer or laptop. Finally, check the ergonomics of your monitors, including adjustability and tilt, to ensure you can position them comfortably.
