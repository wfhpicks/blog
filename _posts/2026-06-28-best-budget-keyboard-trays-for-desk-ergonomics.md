When it comes to setting up a home office, creating an ergonomic workspace is crucial for productivity and comfort. In this article, we'll explore the best budget keyboard trays for desk ergonomics, including cheap under desk keyboard mounts and affordable adjustable keyboard trays.

#

# What to Look For

When shopping for a budget keyboard tray, there are several key factors to consider. First, look for a tray that is adjustable in terms of height and tilt, allowing you to customize the position to fit your needs. Second, consider the size of the tray and whether it can accommodate your keyboard and mouse. Third, think about the material and construction of the tray, opting for a sturdy and durable option that can withstand regular use. Finally, check the compatibility of the tray with your desk and ensure that it is easy to install and remove as needed.
